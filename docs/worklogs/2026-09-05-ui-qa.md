## Findings resolved during the integrated audit

The first opened Ink Snow images exposed real contrast and navigation problems that numeric layout
checks alone did not show:

1. Navigating away from narrow Settings preserved `.app-content.scrollTop = 746`, leaving the Home
   hero entirely above the viewport at `y = -605.625`. The live navigation path now resets the real
   application scroller. The final probe starts with Settings at scrollTop 746, reaches Home at
   scrollTop 0, and finds the hero fully visible at `y = 140.375`.
2. The Models recommendation heading was near-white over the wallpaper's white band. It now has an
   adaptive raised-paper backplate; the final Ink Snow crop is readable without hiding the scene.
3. The first Home identity treatment put only the avatar and eyebrow on a dark strip, leaving the
   headline and subtitle unreadable on Ink Snow and Pistachio. The final identity wrapper keeps the
   portrait, eyebrow, greeting, and supporting copy together in one rounded adaptive panel at both
   390 and 1440 pixels.
4. Home's lower Continue/Working/Projects content, Search results, the Tools permission note, and
   the Projects library heading also lost contrast on bright wallpapers. Their final adaptive local
   backplates keep headings, metadata, and body text readable while leaving substantial wallpaper
   visible.
5. Forced colors initially erased the minimize and close glyphs in the custom title strip. The final
   forced-color capture shows minimize, maximize, and close using the system CanvasText color.

Focused before/after evidence is retained in the same evidence directory. The final matrix files and
`visual-report.json` are authoritative for the corrected renderer state.
